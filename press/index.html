Short bio

Khalid Nasser

Khalid Nasser is a Dubai-based professional boxer and entrepreneur. He won the WBC Middle East Super Lightweight Title in November 2023 and the WBC Asian Boxing Council Silver Super Lightweight Title in April 2024. BoxRec lists his nationality as Pakistan and his birthplace and residence as Dubai.

Long bio

A career built in Dubai

Khalid Nasser’s professional record begins in 2020 and is rooted in Dubai. His championship record includes a fifth-round TKO of Ernest Kapinga for the WBC Middle East Super Lightweight Title in November 2023. In April 2024, BoxRec records another title-winning performance over Aekkawee Kaewmanee for the WBC Asian Silver Super Light title. Gulf Today reported in 2022 that he runs the Shukran water brand.
