# Research summary — Khalid Nasser

Research was completed before implementation. The sites below were reviewed for layout, hero treatment, navigation, statistics, media/press structure, brand integration and mobile-oriented presentation. No code, copy or images were copied.

| Site | Pattern observed | Pattern adopted |
|---|---|---|
| Anthony Joshua | anthonyjoshua.com | Commerce + bold hero imagery | Use image-first hero and restrained brand/press pathways. |
| Tyson Fury | tyson-fury.com | Biography, business, team, merchandise | Use athlete/business split and concise utility navigation. |
| Canelo Alvarez | canelo.com | App-like visual landing | Use cinematic visuals but keep HTML crawlable. |
| Amir Khan | amirkhan.com | Athlete identity and career emphasis | Use achievement-led personal profile. |
| Naseem Hamed | boxing legacy/official references | Legacy and personality-led presentation | Use title/legacy storytelling. |
| Terence Crawford | tbudcrawford.com | Event-first hero + documentary media | Use fight/event spotlight and media archive. |
| Vasyl Lomachenko | lomachenko.com | Boxing-first identity | Keep navigation focused on athlete and record. |
| Conor McGregor | shop.conormcgregor.com | Fight record + biography + business ecosystem | Adopt structured record and entrepreneurship. |
| Cristiano Ronaldo | cristianoronaldo.com | Career highlights + brands | Use highlights and business identity. |
| LeBron James | lebronjames.com | Athlete/business/philanthropy/person split | Use clear 'more than athlete' architecture. |
| Manny Pacquiao | mannypac.com | Next event, stats, press and sponsorship | Use compact stats + press/sponsor routes. |
| Mike Tyson | miketyson.com | Strong personal brand + product ecosystem | Use bold typography and clean brand crossover. |
| Roger Federer | rogerfederer.com | Luxury restraint + stats + partners | Use premium spacing and clean statistics. |
| Lewis Hamilton | lewishamilton.com | Minimal mission-led landing | Keep navigation sparse. |
| Serena Williams | serenawilliams.com | Brand-led content | Separate athlete story from brand ecosystem. |
| Tom Brady | tombrady.com | Editorial publishing | Treat media as an editorial resource. |
| Simone Biles | simonebiles.com | Concise personal-brand presentation | Keep copy direct and image-led. |
| Naomi Osaka | naomiosaka.com | Athlete + culture/business identity | Use image-led topical sections. |
| Neymar Jr. | neymarjr.com | Visual, minimal personal brand | Use large imagery and sparse copy. |

## Verified athlete snapshot

- Current record: **20–1–0, 16 KOs, 21 bouts**, last verified 28 September 2026 from Pakistan Boxing Council and BoxRec.
- BoxRec lists nationality as Pakistan, birthplace and residence as Dubai, United Arab Emirates; orthodox stance; professional debut 12 September 2020.
- WBC reported a fifth-round TKO over Ernest Kapinga for the WBC Middle East Super Lightweight Title on 24 November 2023.
- BoxRec records the 19 April 2024 win over Aekkawee Kaewmanee for the WBC Asian Silver Super Light title.
- WBC Asian Boxing Council September 2026 rankings list Khalid Nasir #1 at Super Lightweight.
- Gulf Today reported in 2022 that Nasser runs Shukran water.
